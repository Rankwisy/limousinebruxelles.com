FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which vehicle should I choose for my group size?
Up to 3 passengers: Mercedes S-Class. Up to 7 passengers: Mercedes V-Class. Up to 8 seats: minibus. Around 50 seats: motorcoach. Beyond that we coordinate several vehicles.
Are your vehicles recent?
Yes. Our fleet is made up of modern vehicles, serviced on a regular schedule and cleaned before every assignment.
Can I rent a vehicle without a driver?
No. Every service includes a professional chauffeur: that is the heart of what we do.
Do the vehicles have Wi-Fi?
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available on board, along with climate control, comfortable seating and generous legroom.
