FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What details do you need for a quote?
The preferred date, the pickup location, the destination, the number of passengers and the type of vehicle you have in mind. Add timings, luggage count and the occasion if you can: the quote will be sharper for it.
How soon will I get a reply?
Within one business day. For an urgent request, say so in your message.
Is the quote free?
Yes, the quote is free and carries no obligation.
How can I reach you?
Through the form on this page or directly by email. All bookings and quotes are handled by email, in English or French.
Can I book from the United States before I travel?
Yes. Most of our international clients book by email weeks ahead of arrival. Send us your flight details and itinerary and we will confirm everything in writing before you fly.
