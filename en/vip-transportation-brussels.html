VIP transportation

The best service is the one you never notice

Brussels hosts a constant flow of delegations, executives, performers and guests whose movements need more than a clean car: they need reliability and silence.

Our VIP chauffeurs are selected for experience and restraint. They plan ahead, they study their routes, they know the discreet entrances at hotels and institutional buildings, and they never comment on what happens in the vehicle.

Depending on the assignment we provide a Mercedes S-Class sedan, a Mercedes V-Class for a small delegation, or several coordinated vehicles for a larger movement. As the political capital of the European Union, Brussels demands this kind of transportation every week — and we are used to its rhythms.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Are your chauffeurs used to high-profile passengers?
Yes. Our VIP chauffeurs are experienced with executives, delegations and distinguished guests, with particular emphasis on discretion and punctuality.
Can you coordinate several vehicles?
Yes. For a delegation or an event we coordinate multiple sedans, minivans, minibuses or motorcoaches according to the number of participants.
Is the service available at night and on weekends?
Yes, seven days a week and at any hour. Give us your timings in the quote request.
How do you guarantee confidentiality?
Our chauffeurs are bound to complete discretion. No information about your movements, your passengers or conversations on board is passed to third parties.
