Mercedes V-Class — built for comfort

The Mercedes V-Class is the benchmark minivan of chauffeured transportation. It delivers what other vehicles force you to trade off: space, comfort and elegance together.

Up to 7 passengers travel in individual seats with generous legroom and a premium interior. The trunk, meanwhile, is genuinely sized for luggage — not for a gym bag.

It is the natural choice for private transfers, family airport runs, small business groups and any journey where you want to stay together without being crammed in. For visitors landing at Brussels Airport with a full set of suitcases, it is almost always what we send.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many passengers does the Mercedes V-Class carry?
Up to 7 passengers, seated individually with generous legroom.
Is there room for luggage?
Yes. The V-Class has a trunk genuinely sized for every passenger’s suitcase — one of its main advantages for airport transfers.
Is the V-Class suitable for long-distance travel?
Very much so. Its suspension comfort, sound insulation and space make it an excellent vehicle for trips to other Belgian cities or neighboring countries.
