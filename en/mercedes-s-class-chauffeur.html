Our flagship vehicles

The definitive luxury sedan

The Mercedes S-Class has nothing left to prove. Near-silent at speed, adaptive suspension, leather seating and exceptional finish: it turns an ordinary journey into a genuinely quiet hour.

Driven by one of our professional chauffeurs in formal attire, it is the natural choice for business meetings, receptions, gala evenings and welcoming your most demanding clients.

Up to 3 passengers travel in the rear with generous space. For a larger group, the Mercedes V-Class offers the same standard with more seats.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many passengers does the Mercedes S-Class carry?
Up to 3 passengers, with particularly generous space in the rear.
Is the S-Class suitable for airport transfers?
Yes, for 1 to 3 passengers with reasonable luggage. For a family or a group with several suitcases we recommend the Mercedes V-Class.
Does the chauffeur wear formal attire?
Yes. Our chauffeurs work in immaculate attire, with a courteous welcome and luggage assistance.
Can I book the S-Class for an evening?
Yes. We offer hourly, evening and full-day hire in addition to point-to-point transfers.
