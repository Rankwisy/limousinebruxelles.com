FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it a bus or a limousine?
Both. Aurum offers several options depending on the number of passengers and the type of trip: limousine, car with chauffeur, Mercedes V-Class minivan, minibus seating up to 8, and a motorcoach of around 50 seats. Describe your journey and we will recommend the right vehicle.
How much does it cost to rent a bus or chauffeured vehicle in Brussels?
As a guide: an 8-seat minibus costs around €350 per day and a 50-seat motorcoach around €600 per day, chauffeur included. For limousines and cars with chauffeur, the rate depends on the route. The final price varies with duration, passenger count and the exact itinerary — request a personalized quote.
How many passengers can you carry?
From 1 to roughly 50. The Mercedes S-Class seats up to 3 passengers, the Mercedes V-Class up to 7, the minibus up to 8, and the motorcoach around 50. For larger groups we coordinate several vehicles.
Do you operate outside Brussels?
Yes. We cover the whole of Belgium — Antwerp, Ghent, Bruges, Liège, Namur, Charleroi, the coast and the Ardennes — as well as neighboring countries (France, the Netherlands, Luxembourg, Germany) on request.
Do you handle airport transfers?
Yes. We serve Brussels Airport (BRU, Zaventem), Brussels South Charleroi (CRL), Liège, Antwerp, plus Lille and Amsterdam Schiphol. Your chauffeur tracks your flight number, meets you on arrival and handles the luggage.
Do you serve visitors coming from the United States?
Yes, regularly. We look after American travelers arriving at Brussels Airport for vacations, EU institution business, conferences and weddings. Quotes and correspondence are handled in English, and your chauffeur will speak English.
How do I book and get a quote?
Fill in the quote form or email us at info@limousinebruxelles.com with the date, pickup location, destination, number of passengers and the type of vehicle you have in mind. You will get a clear proposal within one business day.
Do your vehicles have Wi-Fi and air conditioning?
Yes. Our vehicles are modern and well maintained: comfortable seating, often in leather, free Wi-Fi, generous legroom, climate control and premium comfort throughout.
Do you work with companies?
Yes. Staff shuttles, delegation transfers, conferences, congresses and incentive trips: we work with private clients, companies and event agencies alike, with clear invoicing.
