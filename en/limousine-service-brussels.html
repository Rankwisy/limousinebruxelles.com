Limousine service

Arriving matters as much as leaving

A limousine is not chosen only to get from A to B: it is chosen for the impression it leaves. Our luxury sedans, driven by a chauffeur in formal attire, are made for the moments that count.

Business meetings, gala dinners, embassy receptions, milestone birthdays, welcoming international clients: the vehicle is waiting on time, the chauffeur opens the door, and the journey passes in near silence.

For groups, the same standard applies to our Mercedes V-Class minivans, our minibuses and our VIP coaches. The level of service does not depend on the number of seats.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a limousine and a car with chauffeur?
In practice the service is close: a premium vehicle driven by a professional chauffeur. We use “limousine” for luxury sedans such as the Mercedes S-Class, chosen for occasions where the impression matters most. We do not operate stretch limousines.
How many people fit in the limousine?
Up to 3 passengers in a Mercedes S-Class. Beyond that we recommend the Mercedes V-Class (up to 7 passengers), the minibus (up to 8 seats) or the motorcoach (around 50 seats).
Can I hire a limousine for just a few hours?
Yes. We offer both a simple point-to-point transfer and hourly hire by the hour, half day or full day.
What does a limousine with chauffeur cost?
The rate is quoted individually based on duration, route and passenger count. Describe your trip and you will receive a precise proposal in euros.
